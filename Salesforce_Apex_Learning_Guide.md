## Best Practices
- Avoid SOQL/DML in loops.
- Write bulkified code.
- Reuse code.
- Handle exceptions.
- Write unit tests.
- Enforce security.

## Outcome
Build scalable Apex solutions and prepare for Salesforce Developer interviews.
